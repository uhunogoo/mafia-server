# Mafia Game Rules

## 1. Roles (Optimal: 9-11 players)

**Civilians (Red):** Win through analysis and voting.
*   **Sheriff:** Checks one player's color each night (*Red / Black*). The Don appears Black to him.
*   **Doctor:** Saves one player each night. *Restriction:* cannot heal the same player (including themselves) two nights in a row.
*   **Civilian:** Has no night actions. Votes and analyzes.

**Mafia (Black):** Know each other. Win through murders and deception.
*   **Mafia:** Collectively choose a victim each night.
*   **Don:** The head of the mafia. Additionally checks one player each night to see if they are the **Sheriff**.

---

## 2. Night Phase (Strict Order)

The host announces: *"The city falls asleep."* Actions are performed silently, by touch or gestures.
1.  **Mafia:** Wakes up and points to their victim. *(**First night** is a test night: the mafia only familiarize themselves, no kills!)*
2.  **Don:** Wakes up and checks a player for Sheriff (Host nods "Yes" or shakes head "No").
3.  **Sheriff:** Wakes up and checks a player's color (Host shows "Red" or "Black").
4.  **Doctor:** Wakes up and points to who they are saving. If it matches the mafia's victim, the kill is canceled.

*Host: "The city wakes up."*

---

## 3. Day Phase (Timing)

**1. Announcement:** The host announces who died (or that no one died). The dead are out and **remain completely silent** until the end of the game.

**2. Speeches (1 min each):** Players speak in turn. Interrupting is forbidden.
*   *First speaker rule:* Starting from **Day 2**, the first speaker **must** nominate a candidate for elimination. Each day, the right of the first word shifts to the next player clockwise.
*   Other players during their speeches may add new candidates.

**3. Debate (1-2 min):** Open arguments, reasoning, pressure. A final list of candidates for the vote is formed.

**4. Defense (30 sec each):** Each nominated candidate has 30 seconds for a final word and self-defense.

**5. Voting:** Simultaneous (on the count of "One, two, three"). Vote only for candidates on the list or abstain.
*   *Tie:* If there are multiple leaders — an immediate revote only between them.
*   *Auto-pardon:* If the revote results in another tie — **no one is eliminated**, the day ends, and night falls.

---

## 4. Victory Conditions

*   **Civilian Victory:** All Blacks (Mafia and Don) are eliminated or killed.
*   **Mafia Victory:** The number of living Blacks **equals or exceeds** the number of living Reds (e.g., 2 mafia vs 2 civilians). The game stops immediately, even if it is currently day.

---

## 5. Golden Rules (Etiquette and Fouls)

1.  **Dead don't speak.** Eliminated players do not give hints, show emotions, sigh, or influence the game with gestures.
2.  **No role cards.** Claiming "I'm the Sheriff" or "I'm a Civilian" is allowed (and often necessary), but showing a card, phone, or any other proof is **strictly forbidden** (automatic ejection from the game).
3.  **Secret until the end.** After death or elimination, a player's role is **not revealed** until the entire game is over (to preserve the Sheriff's and Don's logic).
4.  **Silence at night.** Any whispering, tapping, or noise during the night phase is penalized by the host (warning or foul).
